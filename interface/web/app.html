Listening tests
  • No 5.1 yet? Output Virtual surround + headphones: the speakers are simulated at their room positions as heard from where you are (tracked or manual), so every test below works the same. No front/back cues - the surrounds sound to the sides.
  • 1.1 static centering: sit at the seat. identify - each speaker in turn, check name and wiring. center test: toggle Bypass / Static - with Static the noise fuses into one image at your head.
  • 2.1 shifting: Listener Manual, set the spot you will sit at (e.g. x = +0.4), go sit there. phantom center: Static pulls the image into the nearer speaker, Follow puts it back in front of you.
  • 3.1 manual re-centering: drag the target in the top view while listening - the image moves smoothly, no clicks.
  • 3.2 live: Listener Tracking + Follow, move around: the phantom center stays in front of you.

Guidelines used
  • All speakers on one arc around the seat (equal distance) — ITU-R BS.775
  • Center 0° · front L/R ±30° (22–30° allowed) — ITU / Dolby
  • Surrounds ±110° (90–120° allowed), slightly above ear height (+0.3 m here; ITU: ear height, Dolby-style guides: 1–3 ft above)
  • Tweeters at seated ear height; aim (toe-in) every speaker at the seat
  • Mains ~0.6 m (2 ft) off walls; corners build up bass
  • Subwoofer at the front, off-center, off the walls; fine-tune with a subwoofer crawl
  • Mirror left/right pairs; distance differences are compensated by the DSP (delay + level)

Phone as camera
  • Any phone, any OS (also the QIDK): run an IP-camera app on the phone (Android e.g. "IP Webcam"; iPhone: an app serving MJPEG/RTSP), same Wi-Fi, paste its stream URL above. Expect more latency than USB.
  • iPhone on a Mac (Continuity Camera): same Apple ID with 2FA, Wi-Fi + Bluetooth on, iPhone Settings → General → AirPlay & Continuity → Continuity Camera on, iPhone near the Mac, locked, still, landscape. Check Photo Booth's Video menu first. Python/OpenCV may still not list it: macOS only offers Continuity Camera to apps that opt in. A webcam app such as Camo shows the phone as a normal camera instead.
  • Choose the lens (ultra-wide) in the phone app; turn off Center Stage / auto-framing (it moves the view).
Mode fps
Adjustable settings depend on the camera and its driver: Linux webcams usually expose zoom/focus/exposure; on macOS mostly the mode. Phone cameras (Continuity Camera, DroidCam, Camo...) choose the lens - e.g. ultra-wide - in the phone app. A 4:3 mode often shows more than 16:9 (no sensor crop). Changing the camera or its view invalidates the room setup: run Setup again afterwards.
Initializes everything from one capture: sit at your seat facing the camera (on the FC speaker), ideally with some floor in view. A 5 s countdown, a 2 s 1080p capture, then the depth model builds the 3D scan and the calibration. Tracking keeps running.
Room scan (optional, after the setup): a 10 s countdown to step out of the camera view, then the empty room is captured. It replaces the 3D scan, floor and map - nobody hides the floor or what is behind the seat - and keeps the calibration. Don't move the camera in between.
